Where it goes

What your gift funds

EEHEO channels donations directly into frontline health work across Mbale and the Bugisu region.

HIV prevention & PrEP

Expanding access to PrEP options and prevention counseling for key and vulnerable populations.

Screening & testing

Awareness drives and testing for sickle cell disease, hypertension, and diabetes.

Counseling & support

Trained counselors who follow patients through diagnosis, treatment, and ongoing care.
